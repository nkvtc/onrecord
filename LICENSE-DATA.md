## Underlying government material

Source documents and statistics keep their original terms. We redistribute them under
those terms:

| Source | Terms |
|---|---|
| US Federal Register, other US federal works | Public domain in the US (17 U.S.C. § 105) |
| Government of Canada news (canada.ca) | Open Government Licence – Canada |
| Statistics Canada | Statistics Canada Open Licence |
| US Census Bureau trade data (via FRED) | Public domain (US federal work); FRED terms of use |

| Eurostat | Eurostat reuse policy (CC BY 4.0) |
| DOJ Office of the Pardon Attorney | Public domain (US federal work) |
| FEC filings | Public record; see note below |

**FEC data.** Federal law (52 U.S.C. 30111(a)(4)) prohibits using contributor
information from FEC reports to solicit contributions or for commercial purposes. Our
exports omit street addresses and keep names, amounts, dates, city and state, as filed.
Anyone reusing this data must respect that restriction.

When you add an adapter, add its source's terms to this table in the same PR.
